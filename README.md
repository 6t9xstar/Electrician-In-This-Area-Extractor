## Electrician-In-This-Area-Extractor

**Electrician-In-This-Area-Extractor** is a data extraction tool built to collect **publicly available electrician business listings** from “electricians in this area” search results and business directories. The tool helps gather and organize local electrician data into structured formats for research, market analysis, and educational purposes.

### What It Extracts

* Business name
* Service category (electrician / electrical services)
* Location (city, state, address)
* Phone number (if publicly listed)
* Website (if available)
* Ratings & review count
* Other publicly visible business metadata

### Features

* Targets **location-based electrician searches**
* Extracts data from **public business listings**
* Supports multiple locations
* Structured output (CSV / JSON)
* Easy to extend for other local service categories

### Use Cases

* Local market research
* Competitor analysis
* Service area mapping
* Educational scraping projects
* Business intelligence (public data only)
